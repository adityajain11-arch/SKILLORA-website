## Brand & Style

The design system projects a warm, reassuring, and empowering sanctuary for early-career talent and students navigating the internship landscape. The brand personality blends supportive empathy with ambitious optimism—breaking down the intimidating corporate jargon into accessible, friendly milestones.

The design movement centers on a hybrid of **Warm Tactility** and **Modern Soft UI**:
- Tactile, pillowy surfaces that replace rigid enterprise edges with generous radii.
- Delicate multi-stop ambient lighting instead of stark directional shadows, producing soft glowing halos.
- Playful celebratory motifs, including pill badges, pastel confetti accents, and gentle warm-glow states.
- High-empathy micro-interactions that feel responsive and encouraging rather than demanding or clinical.

## Colors

The palette balances gentle, low-stress violet with energizing peach and mint tones to create an approachable career interface.

### Light Mode
- **Canvas / Background**: `#FFF8F0` (Warm Cream). Avoid harsh cold white backdrops.
- **Surfaces & Cards**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) with tinted warm ambient shadows (`rgba(124, 108, 240, 0.08)` and `rgba(255, 138, 101, 0.06)`).
- **Primary Action**: `#7C6CF0` (Soft Violet) for high-intent actions, primary buttons, and focal elements.
- **Secondary Accent**: `#FF8A65` (Peach / Coral) for highlights, milestone tags, and secondary interactive moments.
- **Success / Positive**: `#34D399` (Mint Green) for application accepted states, badges, and progress counters.
- **Text Primary**: `#2D2A3E` (Deep Warm Gray) ensuring WCAG AAA accessibility against cream backdrops.
- **Text Muted**: `#6B6880` for secondary descriptions, timestamps, and subtitles.

### Dark Mode (Cozy Plum-Navy)
- **Canvas / Background**: `#1B1826` (Deep Plum-Navy).
- **Surfaces & Containers**: `#26223A` (Subtle plum container) wrapped with low-contrast borders in `#3D3759`.
- **Primary Action**: `#A99CFF` (Bright Lavender) ensuring high legibility and soft luminosity.
- **Secondary Accent**: `#FFA07A` (Warm Soft Peach).
- **Success / Positive**: `#5EE3B0` (Soft Mint).
- **Text Primary**: `#F2EEFF` (Soft Off-White with lavender undertones).
- **Text Muted**: `#A5A0BA`.

### Semantic Gradients
- **Hero & Celebratory Gradient**: Linear 135deg from `#7C6CF0` to `#FF8A65`.
- **Subtle Surface Glow**: Radial overlay `radial-gradient(circle at 10% 20%, rgba(124, 108, 240, 0.08) 0%, rgba(255, 138, 101, 0.04) 100%)`.

## Typography

The type system is built on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies, using its rounded apexes and geometric proportion to project friendliness without sacrificing structure.

- **Headlines & Displays**: Defined with tight, clean tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) and bold weights to ground each layout with confident clarity.
- **Body Content**: Tuned with generous line heights (`1.5x` to `1.6x`) to make long job descriptions and student reflections relaxed and readable.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Set with semi-bold to bold weights (`600`-`700`) to keep tags, metadata, and status badges legible at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, scaling down to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Form Factors & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers are capped at a maximum width of `1280px` to maintain comfortable readability.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Split-screen cards stack into a unified scroll hierarchy.
- **Mobile (< 767px)**: 4 columns with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Multi-column cards collapse to single-column feeds with edge-to-edge touch targets.

### Spacing Principles
Spacing leans towards airiness and comfort, avoiding dense enterprise tables. Internal card padding strictly uses `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on desktop and `space-md` (`1rem`) on compact displays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses layered ambient glows and warm-tinted shadows instead of cold gray offsets.

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface)**: The warm cream background (`#FFF8F0` in light, `#1B1826` in dark).
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**:
  - *Light Mode*: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(124, 108, 240, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 42, 62, 0.03);`
  - *Dark Mode*: Subtle border `1px solid #3D3759` with zero to slight violet bloom `0 4px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns)**:
  - *Light Mode*: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(124, 108, 240, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(255, 138, 101, 0.08);`
  - *Dark Mode*: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), 0 0 12px rgba(169, 156, 255, 0.12);`
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays)**:
  - Diffused ambient shadow paired with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) using translucent cream (`rgba(255, 248, 240, 0.8)`) or deep plum (`rgba(27, 24, 38, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The shape system relies on playful, comforting curvatures:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards)**: Default to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) up to `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`).
- **Interactive Badges, Pills & Chips**: Use full circular radius (`9999px`) to create smooth pill shapes.
- **Modals & Hero Featured Panels**: Feature deep rounded corners (`1.5rem` / `24px`), reinforcing the welcoming aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#7C6CF0` with white text. Height: `48px` (desktop), `44px` (mobile). Radius: `9999px` (pill) or `16px`. Subtle hover scale (`scale(1.02)`) and warm shadow glow (`0 6px 20px rgba(124, 108, 240, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary**: Tinted soft peach surface with `#FF8A65` text, or transparent stroke button with `1.5px` solid `#7C6CF0`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Neutral text with rounded padding; on hover, fills with translucent violet (`rgba(124, 108, 240, 0.08)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges**: Full border-radius (`9999px`), `space-xs` vertical padding and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- **Role Tags (Remote, Paid, Mentorship)**: Soft mint background (`rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.15)`) with `#10B981` text or warm peach background (`rgba(255, 138, 101, 0.15)`) with `#EA580C` text.

### Cards (Internship Listing & Company Spotlight)
- White surface `#FFFFFF` (light) or `#26223A` (dark) with `20px` to `24px` radius.
- Padding: `1.5rem`.
- Header section includes company avatar (rounded `16px`), title, and salary/stipend pill badge. Hover lifts the card `-3px` with an expanded warm shadow.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Radius: `16px` for standard inputs; `9999px` (pill) for global internship search bars.
- Background: Light cream-gray `#FBF6F0` (light) or `#1E1A2C` (dark).
- Focus state: `2px` ring in `#7C6CF0` with zero harsh black outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Rounded `6px` for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Selected state fills with `#7C6CF0` and displays a white check icon or dot.

### Celebratory Accents & Feedback Modules
- **Application Sent / Milestone Reached**: Triggers lightweight animated micro-confetti using `#7C6CF0`, `#FF8A65`, and `#34D399` pastels.
- **Match Score Indicator**: Rounded ring meter with a violet-to-peach gradient stroke and supportive rating copy (e.g., "Great Fit!").
